# **Pawan Thapa – Portfolio**

Welcome to my portfolio!

I am **Pawan Thapa**, a geospatial scientist with a strong background in **GIS, Remote Sensing, Aerial, Land and Hydro Surveying, Fluvial Geomorphology, and Earth Science**. My work integrates **advanced geospatial analysis, environmental research, and data‑driven methods** to study rivers, landscapes, water systems, and climate‑related environmental change.

This portfolio showcases my **academic, professional, and collaborative projects**, including:

*   Remote sensing applications
*   UAV and field surveying
*   River morphology and flood modeling
*   Machine learning and deep learning for environmental systems
*   GIS automation and geospatial analytics
*   Research publications and teaching materials

It also serves as a **repository** for my independent projects, coursework, tutorials, and technical demonstrations.
